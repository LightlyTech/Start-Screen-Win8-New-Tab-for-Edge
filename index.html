Your new tab, laid out like a Start screen.

Start Screen replaces your new tab page with six columns of large, coloured tiles for the sites you actually visit — each showing its own icon. Right-click a tile to change it, drag one to move it, then get straight back to what you were doing.

What it does

Everything below is in version 1.6.5. None of it changes another website, alters search results, or needs a server.

  • Real site icons

    Each tile pulls the site's own icon through the browser's favicon API. Sites without one get a globe, never a broken image.

  • A grid that fits

    Six columns on every screen, cell size taken from the window height, so a laptop and a large monitor both fill without scrolling.

  • Drag to reorder

    Press and hold any tile, drop it where you want it; the tiles in between slide across so you see the landing spot before letting go.

  • Three tile sizes

    Small, wide or double-height. Pick one in the tile's dialog and the grid re-flows around the new footprint.

  • Edit in one dialog

    Right-click a tile — or use the pencil in the top bar — to change its name, URL, colour from six palettes, or size.

  • Delete with an exit

    The × fades a tile out first, then closes the gap behind it. Restore defaults brings the original sixteen back.

  • Pick from bookmarks

    The add dialog can list your bookmarks, or pages you visited in the last 90 days, and take one of those instead of a typed URL.

  • Search from the top bar

    Type and press Enter to search Bing, matched to your interface language. The magnifier just focuses the field when it is empty.

  • Custom background

    Six gradient presets, or your own photo downscaled to 1920 px and kept on your machine, plus a dim slider so tile names stay readable.

  • Eight languages

    The interface, the default tiles and the search engine all follow your language — switch any time from the globe button.

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Microsoft Edge extension “Start Screen · Win8 New Tab”, version 1.6.5.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

Everything the extension knows stays on your own device. It has no account system, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no remote code. Nothing you do in it is sent to the developer or to anybody else.

What is stored on your device

The extension saves three things so the page looks the same next time:

  • Your tile list: the name, URL, colour, size and position of every tile you add or change.
  • Your background: a named preset gradient, or the image you chose (downscaled to at most 1920 px), plus its dim level.
  • Your interface language.

These live in chrome.storage.local, with localStorage as a fallback. Removing the extension removes them; no copy is kept anywhere else.

What is read, and only while you ask for it

Two permissions exist purely to fill in a URL without typing it. Both are used only while the “pick a site” dialog is open on screen:

  • Bookmarks — read once through chrome.bookmarks.getTree() when you switch that dialog to its bookmarks tab, so you can choose a saved page. The folder tree is flattened in memory; no bookmark is created, changed or deleted.
  • History — read once through chrome.history.search() when you switch the dialog to its history tab, limited to the last 90 days. The result list stays in the page's memory and is thrown away when the dialog closes.

Neither list is stored, logged or transmitted. If you never open that dialog, neither permission is ever exercised.

Network activity

The extension makes no requests of its own: no update ping, no telemetry endpoint, no font or asset download. Site icons come from the browser's own favicon store through the favicon permission. When you open a tile, or press Enter in the search field, your browser loads that page exactly as it would from the address bar.

Permissions, and what each one is for

PermissionWhy it is requested
storageKeeps your tiles, background and language on the device. Without it every new tab would start from scratch.
faviconDraws the real icon of each site on its tile. Only the icon of a URL you added is requested.
bookmarksLets you pick a saved page instead of typing its URL, while the add dialog is open.
historyLets you pick a page you visited recently, while that dialog is open.

What it never does

  • No collection, sale, sharing or transfer of personal data to anyone.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No remote code: all code ships inside the package, as the Manifest V3 rules for this store require.
  • No content scripts: no page you visit is read, modified or injected into.
  • No access to passwords, form data, other extensions, or anything beyond the four permissions above.

Children

This is a general-purpose browser customisation. It is not directed to children under 13 and collects personal data from nobody, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and its date are updated before that version is published, and the change is described in the listing's release notes.
